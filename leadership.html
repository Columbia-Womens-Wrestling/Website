Coaching Staff

Head Coach
Maya Letona
CC '24

Maya Letona is a three-time All-American who wrestled for Columbia's Women's Wrestling Club, competing internationally at 62 kg. She was a member of the U.S. Senior National Team and competed at the U.S. Olympic Trials for Team USA. She finished fourth at the 2023 Senior Nationals. A Santa Cruz, California native, she took up wrestling in 2020 at the NYC Regional Training Center and is now a USA Wrestling certified coach. After graduating from Columbia College in 2024, she returned to the program as Head Coach.

Volunteer Assistant Coach
Emma Randall

Emma Randall coaches at Columbia and the NYC Regional Training Center. She holds the USA Wrestling Gold level certification, held by fewer than 100 coaches in the United States. Emma competed at the national level before moving into coaching.

Chloe Ayres

Chloe Ayres was a three-time New Jersey state champion and Fargo All-American at Princeton, where she also earned two-time Under-23 All-American honors. She now coaches at Beat the Streets New York and volunteers as an assistant coach with CWWC.

Vayle Baker

Vayle Baker is a two-time Cadet World Team member and five-time national freestyle champion. She wrestled collegiately at Augsburg and King University, earning NCWWC All-American honors in 2023. Vayle now volunteers as an assistant coach with CWWC.
