Recruit Me

If you're a high school or transfer wrestler interested in Columbia, submit the Recruit Me form and our coaching staff will be in touch.

Columbia Women's Wrestling competes on a Division I schedule against NCAA and NAIA varsity programs. Last year, 75 completed Recruit Me forms led to 50 formal applications and 12 acceptances — 10 of whom are now first-year students on campus.
